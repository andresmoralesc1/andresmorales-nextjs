# SEO Content Brief — ES Blog Posts (Colombia / LATAM)

Author: Andrés Morales · Site: andresmorales.com.co · Last updated: 2026-09-04

## Why these posts

The site has technical SEO covered (canonical, hreflang, JSON-LD, sitemap,
robots). What's missing is **topical depth in Spanish** — the EN blog
carries 2 posts, the ES blog has none. The /es/ pages rank for
brand-name queries but are missing from the SERPs for the actual purchase
intent keywords Colombian founders type into Google.

We trade for these four posts first because they target
**commercial/transactional intent** keywords with low competition in
Spanish (vs. saturated English SERPs). Each post is also a candidate to
become a service-page funnel — they end with a "Book a 30-min call"
CTA and a "Ver servicios" link, both tracking to Umami + GA4 once
deployed.

## Production workflow

1. Escribir cada post en `content/blog/es/<slug>.mdx` (siguiendo el frontmatter que usa `listPosts()` en `lib/blog.ts`).
2. Traducir el H1 a un `summary` para Open Graph.
3. Correr `next build` para verificar que el post aparece en `/es/blog` y genera metadata correcta.
4. Hacer commit por separado por cada post (no monolítico).
5. Una vez publicado, enviar a indexar en GSC: `URL Inspection > Request Indexing`.

## Tracking

- Cada CTA de cada post → `track('blog_cta_click', { post: slug, cta: name })` en Umami.
- Comparar dwell time de posts ES vs EN en GSC > Performance.
- Mover los que mejor convierten a la home como "Recent writing" si rinden bien.

## Definition of done

- 4 posts publicados

- Cada uno con 2+ internal links al cluster de servicios
- GSC muestra 0 errores de cobertura en /es/blog/*

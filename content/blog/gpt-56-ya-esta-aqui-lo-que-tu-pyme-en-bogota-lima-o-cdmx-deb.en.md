## Why this matters for your SMB

GPT-5.6 is OpenAI's newest family. Three changes that affect SMBs:

1. **Lower cost per token** — roughly 30% cheaper than GPT-4o for everyday tasks.
2. **Better at structured output** — JSON mode is more reliable, fewer retries.
3. **Multilingual is genuinely good now** — Spanish, Portuguese, and English all perform similarly.

For a small business, the question is whether the savings and quality lift justify migrating your existing automations.

## My recommendation

If your current stack is GPT-4o or GPT-4-turbo and it's working, **wait 60–90 days** before migrating. The ecosystem catches up — n8n nodes, LangChain bindings, vendor docs — over the first quarter.

If you're starting fresh today, build on GPT-5.6 from day one. The cost difference pays for itself inside the first month on any workflow doing more than 100 runs/day.
